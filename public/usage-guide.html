Poultry Management System

Comprehensive Usage Guide for the Web Version

Getting Started

Welcome to the Poultry Management System! This guide will walk you through the core functionalities of the application, helping you to efficiently manage your farm's daily operations.

Navigation

Use the left-hand sidebar to navigate between different modules like Dashboard, Flocks, Eggs, and Finance.

Workspaces

If you manage multiple farms, you can switch between them using the Workspace switcher in the top navigation bar.

Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a high-level, real-time overview of your farm's performance.

  • Key Metrics: View total active birds, daily egg production rates, and recent feed consumption.
  • Financial Summary: Quickly check revenue vs. expenses for the current month.
  • Quick Actions: Use the dashboard shortcuts to log eggs or feed quickly without navigating away.

Flock (Chicken) Management

Keep a detailed record of your birds, organized by batches or houses.

Add a Batch

Click "Add Batch" to register a new group of day-old chicks or pullets. Specify breed, quantity, and arrival date.

Mortality Logging

Record daily mortality to keep your live bird count accurate, which automatically adjusts feed calculation needs.

Egg Production Tracking

Monitor your daily collection to ensure optimal laying percentages.

  • Log Collection: Enter the number of good eggs, cracked, and broken eggs daily.
  • Production Rate: The system automatically calculates the laying percentage based on your active bird count.
  • Grading: Categorize eggs by size (Small, Medium, Jumbo) for better pricing.

Feed & Inventory Management

Never run out of essential supplies with automated tracking.

Feed Consumption

Log daily feed given to each batch. The system tracks grams per bird to help spot underfeeding or waste.

Inventory Alerts

Set low-stock thresholds for feed types and medications. The system will alert you when it's time to reorder.

Financial Tracking & Sales

Manage your farm's profitability with integrated accounting.

  • Record Sales: Log sales of eggs, mature birds, or manure. Generate invoices for your customers.
  • Track Expenses: Record purchases of feed, medication, equipment, and staff salaries.
  • Profit/Loss Reports: Generate automated P&L statements over custom date ranges.

Health Records & CCTV

Ensure bio-security and bird welfare.

Vaccination Schedule

Log administered vaccines and set reminders for upcoming boosters.

CCTV Monitoring

If integrated, view live feeds from your poultry houses directly from the dashboard to monitor bird behavior remotely.

Staff & Contact Management

Organize your team and external relationships.

  • Staff Directory: Keep track of employees, their roles, and attendance.
  • Contacts: Maintain a database of your suppliers (feed, meds) and regular customers for quick access during sales.
