End-User Operational & Farm Workflows Guide

Step-by-step master operational guide for farm managers, supervisors, and attendants. Details daily egg collection logging, nesting box audits, feed stock tracking, health schedules, sales invoicing, and customer payment links.

1. Daily Egg Laying Collections & 30-Egg Crate Calculator (`/dashboard/eggs`)

Logging daily egg collection is vital for calculating laying percentages and predicting farm revenue:

  1. Navigate to `/dashboard/eggs` and click '+ Log Daily Lay'.
  2. Select the collection date and select the target layer flock batch ID (e.g., `B301`).
  3. Input total Good Eggs, Broken/Cracked Eggs, and Spoilt Eggs.
  4. The system automatically computes the 30-egg crate count using `Total Crates = Math.floor(Good Eggs / 30)` and logs remaining loose eggs.
  5. Update Nesting Box Cushion Audits to track padding cleanliness and aggressively reduce shell breakage rates.

2. Livestock Batch Management & Mortality Logging (`/dashboard/chickens`)

Manage layer, broiler, and pullet flocks through their complete lifecycle:

  1. Register new batches under `/dashboard/chickens` with breed name (e.g. Isa Brown, Cobb 500), purchase date, initial count, and cost.
  2. When bird mortality occurs, click 'Log Mortality'. Input date, mortality count, and select root cause (Heat Stress, Infectious Disease, Culling, or Natural Causes).
  3. The system automatically deducts mortality count from the live active bird KPI cards on the main dashboard.
  4. Execute inter-pen bird transfers to reassign flocks between coops when birds mature.

3. Feed Inventory & Daily Consumption Logging (`/dashboard/feed`)

Prevent feed stockouts and track Feed Conversion Ratios (FCR):

  1. Log daily feed usage under `/dashboard/feed` by entering feed type (Layer Mash, Broiler Starter, Grower, Finisher) and bags/Kg consumed per batch.
  2. When inventory levels drop below safety stock thresholds, the system triggers a red low-stock alert banner on the main telemetry dashboard.
  3. View calculated FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio) metrics to evaluate feed efficiency per bird and per crate of eggs.

4. Sales Invoicing & Digital WhatsApp Payment Links (`/dashboard/sales`)

Create professional customer invoices and collect online payments:

  1. Click '+ Record New Sale' under `/dashboard/sales`. Select customer name, product type (e.g., 50 Crates Large Eggs), unit price, and total amount.
  2. The system generates a unique digital invoice with line-item breakdown.
  3. Click 'Generate Pay Link' to create a hosted Paystack or Stripe checkout URL (`/pay-invoice/[id]`).
  4. Click 'Share to WhatsApp' to send the invoice link directly to the customer's phone for immediate payment.
