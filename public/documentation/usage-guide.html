Operational Field Manual

Everyday Attendant & Field Worker Operations Manual

Simple, visual, step-by-step button click instructions for flock attendants, egg collectors, and coop managers. Complete routine duties quickly on mobile or desktop without seeing complex financial ledgers.

Daily Routine

What Staff Members Do in PFMS

Staff attendants are the operational heartbeat of the poultry farm. Your role focuses on logging physical daily activities: morning egg collections, feed distribution per pen, health observations, and room temperature checks. The simplified staff interface prevents accidental edits to billing or accounting.

Duty 1

Logging Daily Egg Collection & Quality Grading

1
Step 1: Open Egg Production Page Click Eggs on your left sidebar menu (or tap Eggs on mobile).
2
Step 2: Click the '+ Log Production' Button Click the primary button marked + Log Production at the top right of the table.
3
Step 3: Fill Collection Quantities
  • Date: Defaults to today's date.
  • Flock Batch & Pen: Select the flock batch you collected from (e.g. Layers Pen A).
  • Good Eggs: Total count of clean, marketable eggs (e.g. 900 eggs = 30 full crates).
  • Damaged / Spoilt Eggs: Total count of cracked, broken, or deformed eggs (e.g. 6 eggs).
4
Step 4: Click 'Save Production Record' Click the green Save button. The total egg inventory updates immediately across the farm.

Duty 2

Feeding Distribution & Stock Usage Logs

1
Recording Feed Provided to Birds Click Feed in the sidebar. Click + Log Feed Consumption. Choose feed type (Layers Mash, Broiler Finisher), enter bags or kilograms distributed to the troughs, and select the pen coop.
2
Logging Feed Bags Received (Restock) When a truck delivers feed, click + Add Stock, enter the number of bags placed into the feed store, and submit.

Duty 3

Mortality & Health Incident Logging

1
Logging Dead Birds Navigate to /dashboard/chickens. Click + Log Mortality. Select the affected flock batch and enter the count of dead birds removed during morning inspection.
2
Recording Symptoms for the Farm Manager Type any visible symptoms into the notes box (e.g. drooping wings, blood in droppings, coughing). When you save, the live batch population adjusts automatically and an alert is flagged for the farm vet.

Duty 4

Coop Pen Inspection, Temperature & Hygiene

🌡️ Temperature & Humidity

Check pen thermometer. Record morning and afternoon temperature. High temperatures ($>32^\circ\text{C}$) trigger cooling alarms.

💧 Drinker & Nipple Line Check

Inspect water pressure, clean bell drinkers, and check for leaks to prevent wet litter ammonia buildup.

🧹 Litter Hygiene & Turning

Log litter rake status and bedding replacements to ensure dry, parasite-free living conditions for hens.

Duty 5

Daily Attendant Shift Checklist (Opening & Closing)

🌅 Morning Shift Routine (6:00 AM – 12:00 PM)
  • Turn on coop lighting and check water lines.
  • Inspect pens for dead birds and log mortality immediately.
  • Distribute morning feed rations to troughs.
  • Execute first egg collection (approx. 10:00 AM).
  • Log collected eggs and damaged egg counts.
🌇 Evening Shift Routine (2:00 PM – 6:00 PM)
  • Execute second egg collection (approx. 3:00 PM).
  • Top up water drinkers and check medication tanks.
  • Inspect pen temperature and adjust ventilation shutters.
  • Lock feed stores and verify predator exclusion barriers.
  • Sign off shift log in the staff portal.
