Staff Access Scope

What a Staff Member Does

As a Staff Attendant, your daily job is to log physical farm activities. You can record daily egg collections, log bird mortality, record feed usage, and check coop housing capacity. You do not need to worry about accounting, setting prices, or adding new workers — your Admin handles that!

Staff Task 1

How to Record Daily Egg Collections

1
Click 'Egg Production' on your Left Menu Look at the left side of your screen and click on Egg Production.
2
Click the purple '+ Log Daily Lay' Button At the top right of your screen, click the button marked + Log Daily Lay.
3
Type Egg Count & Click 'Save Log' Enter total Good Eggs and Cracked Eggs. The system automatically converts them into 30-egg crates! Click Save Log.
2 Click + Log Daily Lay
