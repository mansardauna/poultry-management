Section 1

Easy Setup & Quickstart Onboarding Protocol

Follow these 6 steps to configure your database, setup housing pens, register flock batches, and begin daily logging.

Step 01 /signup & /login

Account Registration & Auth Portal

Create your farm administrator account at `/signup`. Select your billing region (NGN / West Africa or International USD). Log in securely at `/login` to initialize your farm workspace.

Step 02 /setup

Database Engine Selection

Navigate to `/setup`. Choose your database driver: Supabase PostgreSQL (Managed), Standard PostgreSQL (Self-Hosted), or MySQL / MariaDB. The wizard runs an automated connection ping test before proceeding.

Step 03 /dashboard/housing

Housing & Pen Configuration

Define your physical farm infrastructure under `/dashboard/housing`. Create pens, coops, and brooders with maximum bird capacity limits, temperature sensors, and ventilation climate status.

Step 04 /dashboard/chickens

Flock Batch Registration

Register your active livestock batches under `/dashboard/chickens`. Input batch IDs (e.g. B301), breed types (Isa Brown layers, Cobb 500 broilers), initial quantity, age in weeks, and target coop assignment.

Step 05 /dashboard/eggs & /feed

Daily Operational Logging

Attendants log daily egg collections (good vs cracked eggs converted to 30-egg crates) and feed consumption in Kilograms. Low-stock feed alerts automatically trigger when levels drop below safety margins.

Step 06 /dashboard/settings

Subscription & Payment Link Setup

Configure Paystack and Stripe merchant keys in `/dashboard/settings`. Upgrade to Commercial Pro (₦15,000/mo) or Enterprise Plus (₦45,000/mo) to unlock multi-branch matrix analytics and CCTV WebRTC streams.

Core Module 01

Main Telemetry Dashboard & AI Alert Hub

Route: /dashboard | User Roles: Admin, Manager

Real-Time Analytics

OPERATIONAL & TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS:

The Main Telemetry Dashboard is the primary operational command center. It aggregates real-time metrics across all active flock batches, egg laying production, feed stock reserves, and financial ledgers.

  • Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Telemetry Cards: Displays Live Active Birds (with flock mortality rate %), Daily Egg Output (converted to standard 30-egg crates), Feed Inventory Stock Remaining (in Kilograms with low-stock warnings), and Reconciled Net Operational Profit.
  • Guided Farm Setup & Onboarding Progress Widget: A 4-step progress tracker for brand new accounts. Displays progress bar percentage and direct action buttons to configure farm branches, set up pens, register flocks, and log initial egg collection.
  • System Alert Logs (Roster): Real-time event log streaming critical notifications such as low feed stock alerts, spike in mortality incidents, pending staff payroll disbursements, and invoice payment receipts.
  • Global Command Palette & Search Bar: Instant fuzzy search across all 13 application modules, staff rosters, batch IDs, customer contact directories, and invoice records.

Core Module 04

Egg Production, Daily Collections & Nest Audits

Route: /dashboard/eggs | User Roles: Admin, Manager, Staff

30-Egg Crate Calculator

OPERATIONAL & TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS:

The Egg Production module provides comprehensive daily laying collection tracking, breakage audit metrics, crate conversions, and pullet maturation progress indicators.

  • Daily Collection Logger: Form for logging collection date, batch assignment ID, total good eggs collected, broken/cracked eggs, and spoilt eggs.
  • Automated 30-Egg Crate Conversion: System automatically calculates crate count using `Total Crates = Math.floor(Good Eggs / 30)` and displays remaining loose eggs.
  • Nesting Box Cushioning Audits: Audit logging for nesting box padding cleanliness to reduce shell breakage rates.
  • Newly Laying Birds Maturation Progression: Tracks egg weight bounds (grams) and size progression as pullets enter their first laying cycle.

Core Module 15

Account Settings, Subscriptions & Payment Gateways

Route: /dashboard/settings | User Roles: Admin

Paystack & Stripe Sync

OPERATIONAL & TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS:

The Settings & Subscription hub manages multi-tenant workspace credentials, plan upgrade modals, payment gateway keys, and danger zone workspace deletion.

  • Subscription Tier Matrix: Upgrade between Free Starter (1 Branch, 2 Staff, basic logs), Commercial Pro (₦15,000/mo, 5 Branches, AI Voice auto-logger, CCTV predator alerts), and Enterprise Plus (₦45,000/mo, Unlimited Matrix, White-Labeling, REST API tokens, 24/7 Vet Hotline).
  • Merchant Payment Gateway Key Integration: Input Paystack Public/Secret Keys and Stripe Publishable/Secret Keys to accept customer payment for wholesale egg crate sales and live bird invoices.
  • Alert Rules & Thresholds: Customize minimum feed inventory safety stock threshold (Kg) and maximum acceptable mortality rate % per batch before triggering emergency notifications.
