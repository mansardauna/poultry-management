Super Admin Setup & Onboarding Master Guide

What to do immediately after signing up as Platform Super Admin (`owner@poultry.com`)

Post-Signup Step 1

Super Admin Registration & Master Account Creation

Initialize your SaaS platform owner credentials.

1
Click 'Sign Up Free' on Top Nav Bar Click the purple Sign Up Free button on the landing page header.
2
Type Super Admin Email & Password Type your master superadmin email (`owner@poultry.com`), select your currency region (NGN or USD), set your password, and click Create Account.
1 Click Sign Up Free

Figure 1.1: Live Super Admin signup portal.

Post-Signup Step 2

Database Engine Driver Selection & Connection Test

1
Select Database Driver (Supabase / Postgres / MySQL) If setting up a self-hosted instance, choose your database driver. Supabase PostgreSQL is recommended for automatic multi-tenant Row-Level Security.
2
Click 'Test Connection' Click Test Connection to run an automated connection guard ping verification.
Post-Signup Step 3

Configure Paystack / Stripe Keys & Pricing Plans

1
Click 'Settings' on Left Sidebar Input your merchant payment keys (Paystack Secret Key & Stripe Secret Key) so customer invoices generate online checkout links.

2 Plan Tiers

Homepage CMS Content & Headlines Editor

1
Click 'Super Admin CMS' on Top Nav Log in as Super Admin (`owner@poultry.com`) and click the purple Super Admin CMS button.
2
Click 'CMS Content Editor' Edit your landing page hero headlines, feature highlights, and support email settings live!
