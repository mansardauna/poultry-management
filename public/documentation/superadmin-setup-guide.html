Part 1: Initial Deployment

The 5-Step /setup Installation Wizard

When you deploy PFMS onto a new server or domain (e.g. pms.ngeggs.com), the setup wizard located at /setup automates initial database provisioning, seeds master Super Admin credentials, connects payment gateways, and initializes the platform configuration.

Step-by-Step Installation Flow

1
Step 1: Database Engine Selection & Live Connection Guard Choose between ⚡ Supabase Cloud PostgreSQL, 🐘 Standard PostgreSQL, or 🐬 MySQL / MariaDB. Enter your host, port, database name, user, and password. The system executes a live database ping test to verify credentials before unlocking Step 2.
2
Step 2: Master Super Admin Credentials & Platform Identity Configure the master Super Admin email (e.g. owner@poultry.com or superadmin@pfms.com) and a strong password ($\ge 8$ characters). Set default platform name and primary currency symbol (₦, $, €, £, KSh, GH₵).
3
Step 3: Payment Gateways (Paystack & Stripe) Enter Paystack keys (pk_test/live and sk_test/live) for African card and bank transfer checkout. Enter Stripe keys (pk_test/live and sk_test/live) for global USD transactions. These can also be configured post-launch in the Admin Portal.
4
Step 4: Transactional Email Gateway & Plan Defaults Configure SMTP email server or Resend API key to send automated invoice links, password reset emails, and daily shift alerts. Confirm starter subscription tier pricing defaults.
5
Step 5: Final Verification & Instant Launch Review your deployment manifest summary. Click ⚡ Complete Installation & Save Config. The system saves data/database.config.json, executes schema table verification, seals the setup endpoint, and redirects to /dashboard/admin.

Part 2: Master Governance

The Super Admin Control Center (`/dashboard/admin`)

The Super Admin portal is divided into 6 master management tabs. Each tab governs a specific operational layer of your SaaS platform.

Tab 1 of 6

Platform Telemetry, Revenue Analytics & System KPIs

🏢 Total Tenant Farms

Real-time count of all registered farm organizations with active workspace allocations across your entire cluster.

💳 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

Calculated sum of all active commercial Pro and industrial Enterprise subscription billings with automated currency conversion.

📈 Tenant Growth & Revenue Curves

Interactive Recharts visual graphs charting 12-month tenant customer acquisition vs. monthly subscription revenue.

🩺 System Diagnostics Telemetry

Live monitoring of Node.js server heap memory, active database engine connection health, and database response latency.

Tab 5 of 6 (Critical Capability)

Tenant Organizations & Secure Impersonation ("Login as Tenant")

The Tenant Organizations tab provides complete administrative control over all customer farms registered on the platform.

1
Tenant Directory & Search Instantly search tenant farms by name, owner email, subscription tier, or organization ID. View status badges (Active, Suspended, Expired, Inactive).
2
Manual Subscription Override & Upgrades Click Upgrade / Manage on any tenant to manually elevate them to Commercial Pro or Enterprise Plus, extend subscription validity, or set a custom expiration date without processing a card transaction.
3
⚡ One-Click Tenant Impersonation ("Login as Tenant")

Click the Login as Tenant button next to any registered farm. You will be instantly logged into their live dashboard with full Farm Admin permissions — without needing their password.

🛡️ Security Safeguard:

The session is cryptographically signed with impersonatedBy: 'superadmin'. A persistent purple banner is displayed across the top of the screen: "Viewing Tenant: [Farm Name] — Exit Impersonation". Clicking Exit Impersonation immediately terminates the tenant session and restores your Super Admin workspace.

4
Tenant Suspension & Deletion Suspend non-compliant accounts to block login access, or permanently purge tenant organizations and all associated workspaces with confirmation safeguards.
Tab 6 of 6

Security, Credential Rotation & Maintenance Mode

🔑 Master Credential Rotation

Update master Super Admin login email and password anytime from the Settings tab. Changes are immediately hashed using bcrypt with salt rounds 10.

🔒 Two-Factor Authentication (2FA / TOTP)

Enforce Time-Based One-Time Password (TOTP) verification using Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or 1Password for all Super Admin logins.

🚧 Global Platform Maintenance Mode

Toggle Maintenance Mode during software upgrades or database migrations. Regular users and staff attempting to access the platform receive a polite maintenance screen with your custom message, while Superadmins retain full bypass access.
