Admin Privileges

What an Admin Can Do (That Staff Cannot)

As a Farm Admin or Business Owner, you have full control over your farm workspace. Unlike regular Staff members who can only enter daily counts, Admins can add and delete workers, assign passwords, approve salary payroll, manage customer invoicing, configure payment gateways, and manage multi-farm branches.
