Farm Management Manual

Farm Owner & Admin Operational Governance Manual

Comprehensive visual guide for commercial farm owners and enterprise managers governing flock lifecycles, egg yield metrics with sub-period selectors, feed threshold alerts, public online invoice settlement, and multi-branch operations.

Governance Scope

Farm Admin Responsibilities & Privileges

Farm Admins hold full commercial and operational authority over their farm workspace. Admins configure pen houses, assign flock batches, monitor egg yield and mortality trends, manage customer sales and invoices, track feed conversions, disburse staff payroll, and review financial ledgers.

Module 1

Flock Batches, Pen Houses & Hen-Day Production

1
Creating a New Flock Batch Navigate to /dashboard/chickens. Click + Add Batch. Enter batch name (e.g. Layers Batch B-4), bird breed (Isa Brown, Bovans Brown, Lohmann White), acquisition date, initial quantity, cost per bird, and assign to a specific pen coop.
2
Pen House Allocation & Capacity Management Navigate to /dashboard/housing to manage pen structures. Monitor bird density per square meter to prevent overcrowding and maintain optimal ventilation.
3
Hen-Day Egg Production Rate (HDEP%) The system automatically calculates daily Hen-Day Rate: HDEP% = (Good Eggs Collected ÷ Live Hens Remaining in Batch) × 100 Commercial benchmark target is between 85% to 94% peak lay.

Module 2

Egg Production Analytics & Dynamic Sub-Period Selectors

1
Interactive Sub-Period Chart Selector Both the Dashboard and Egg analytics charts feature dynamic sub-period dropdown selectors:
  • Weekly View: Choose between the last 8 weeks (e.g. Current Week (Oct 1 – Oct 7) or Last Week (Sep 24 – Sep 30)).
  • Monthly View: Select any month of the current or historical year.
  • Yearly View: Analyze full 12-month annualized production volumes.
  • All History: Quick span filters for All Recorded History, Past 30 Days, Past 14 Days, or Past 7 Days.
2
Quality Grading & Breakage Control Every collection entry records both Good Eggs (marketable trays) and Damaged / Spoilt Eggs (cracked, soft-shelled, or deformed). Track breakage percentages to detect calcium deficiencies in feed or rough handling during collection.

Module 3

Feed Inventory, Restock & Low-Stock Threshold Alerts

Feed represents up to 70% of poultry operational expenses. The feed manager at /dashboard/feed ensures complete inventory visibility.

🌾 Feed Stock Inflow (Restocking)

Record supplier deliveries with batch code, bag weight (e.g. 25kg or 50kg), unit purchase price, and supplier name.

🥣 Daily Consumption Logs

Track daily feed consumed per pen coop. Monitor average grams per bird per day (standard: 110g – 125g per layer).

⚠️ Low-Stock Threshold Guard

Set custom minimum stock thresholds. When bags drop below the limit, the dashboard raises an immediate high-priority warning.

📊 Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)

Calculates kilograms of feed required to produce 1 tray or kilogram of eggs, identifying feeding waste immediately.

Module 4 (Major Feature)

Sales Ledger, Paystack/Stripe Invoices & Public Payment Links

1
Creating Commercial Invoices Navigate to /dashboard/sales. Click + Create Invoice. Select customer contact, specify items (Egg Trays, Spent Layers, Broiler Birds, Organic Manure), unit rates, quantities, and optional discounts.
2
Public Invoice Payment Link (/pay-invoice/[id]) Every invoice generates a shareable public payment link (e.g. https://pms.ngeggs.com/pay-invoice/inv_12345). You can copy this link and send it directly to your buyer via WhatsApp, SMS, or Email.
3
Automated Settlement vs. Offline Verification

When a customer opens the payment link, they have two payment options:

  • Online Checkout (Paystack / Stripe): Customer pays via Debit Card, Apple Pay, or USSD. Upon successful transaction, Paystack/Stripe webhooks automatically mark the invoice as Paid, credit your sales ledger, and generate an instant payment receipt.
  • Offline Bank Transfer: Customer submits bank payment reference. The invoice status is set to Pending Verification. A Farm Admin must verify the funds in their physical bank statement before confirming settlement — preventing unauthorized self-marking.
4
Replay Attack & Reference Idempotency Protection Each payment transaction reference is permanently indexed and validated. The server strictly rejects reused references across different invoices, closing replay risks.

Module 6

Staff Access Control, Roles & Shift Rosters

1
Adding Staff Attendants Navigate to /dashboard/staff. Click + Add Staff Member. Enter full name, assigned role (Staff Attendant, Farm Manager, Resident Vet), contact phone, and login password.
2
Role-Based Access Boundaries Staff logins are strictly isolated from sensitive data. Attendants can access operational logging (Eggs, Feed, Health, Pen Inspection), but cannot access Financial Ledgers, Sales Invoices, or Farm Settings.

Module 7

Health Diagnostics, Vaccination Calendar & Mortality Logs

💉 Vaccination Schedules

Track Gumboro, Newcastle (ND Lasota / Komarov), Marek's, and Fowl Typhoid schedules with automated countdown alerts before due date.

🪦 Mortality Logs & Symptom Tracking

Record daily mortality per batch with suspected cause (Heat stress, Coccidiosis, Respiratory infection). Spikes over 0.5% trigger high-priority alerts.

Module 8

Enterprise Multi-Branch Workspaces & Centralized Fleet

For commercial producers operating multiple farm sites (e.g. North Branch (Broilers), South Branch (Layers)), Enterprise mode at /dashboard/enterprise provides multi-branch orchestration.

  • Instant Workspace Switcher: Switch active farm branches in the sidebar without logging out.
  • Aggregated Feed Pooling: Order bulk grain deliveries to a central depot and transfer allocations across branches.
  • Consolidated Group Financials: View rollups of egg production, total live birds, and sales revenue across all branch locations simultaneously.
